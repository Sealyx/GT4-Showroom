GT4 SHOWROOM - IMPORTANT CHANGES SINCE v2.5.0
Current source: local white-paint, framing and independent presentation preset update
Updated: 21 November 2026
Comparison baseline: gt4showroom-v2.5.0 Git tag

This is a consolidated changelog of important player-facing additions and fixes,
not a list of every intermediate test build or formatting change
The historical sections below describe the final v3.0 checkout.
The additions dated 19 November use the local Codex build sources.

ADDITIONS SINCE THE PROLOGUE STYLE UPDATE - 19 NOVEMBER 2026

Prologue style
- Added Default and Prologue choices to Showroom Settings, saved between sessions.
- Prologue combines its cloud backdrop, floor presentation, thumbnail frames
  and circular paint selection with smooth transitions between styles.
- Added a separate cloud rendering pass over a blue sky, using illuminated
  volume density and bloom from bright cloud regions.
- Puffy clouds and thinner distant cloud bands drift left at different speeds.
- Removed vertical cloud movement and increased the approved drift speed by 40%.
- Increased cloud bloom following the final visual review.
- Added thicker rounded dark-gray thumbnail frames, stronger drop shadows,
  and a glowing gold highlight for the selected thumbnail.
- Enlarged glossy circular paint chips with a pulsing yellow selection ring.
- Prologue vehicle shadows stay beneath the vehicle and follow its rotation.

Prologue paint highlight revision - approved 19 November 2026
- Sped up the orange highlight cycle to 0.75 seconds.
- Removed the static gray paint-chip rim.
- Added a thinner orange stroke and a stronger halo centered on its edge.
- Reduced the color-circle size to leave a clear gap inside the highlight.
- Removed paint-circle shadows and raised Prologue chips and their hit targets slightly.

Prologue paint size and camera fitting - approved 19 November 2026
- Enlarged Prologue circular paint chips by approximately 44% with proportional spacing.
- Kept orange stroke thickness and the approved pulse and glow behavior.
- Moved the glint and hit targets with the enlarged circles.
- Fixed camera fitting following the animated thumbnail strip during paint
  and livery selection, which caused the vehicle to move down.
- Renderer regression checks preserved camera position, zoom, vehicle angle
  and scale at identical animation times in both styles. GTA testing remains pending.

Background settings and automatic floor reflections - approved 19 November 2026
- Hid Background in Prologue and blocked access to its choices.
- Remembered the Default background choice across style changes.
- Enabled floor reflections when entering Prologue or Red and disabled them
  for Default and Dark Gray when switching presentation.
- Applied automatic changes once per presentation change, preserving manual overrides.
- Remembered the last enabled floor-reflection quality independently of Off.
- Preserved vehicle-reflection settings and legacy saved preferences.
- Regression checks passed for presentation changes, category changes, manual
  overrides, remembered quality, legacy settings and Background visibility.
- Compiled and reviewed with the standalone renderer; GTA testing remains pending.

Accessory-aware main-showroom framing - approved 19 November 2026
- Measured horizontal body sections to distinguish broad vehicle bodies and
  cabs from narrower rooftop accessories, without model-specific new rules.
- Used the body envelope to reduce accessory-driven camera distance penalties.
- Bounded the closer view using the full rotating accessory envelope.
- Preserved the baseline camera position, angle and entry/rotation timing.
- Retained existing approved accessory corrections and thumbnail behavior.
- Strengthened the universal correction after reviewing the closer Mr. Whoopee.
- Verified settled full-turn geometry visibility on 24 installed model
  configurations in Default and Prologue at 1920x1080 and 1440x1080.
- Compared camera position, heading and geometry scale across 1,947 frames;
  the change adjusts magnification while preserving those parameters.
- Compiled and reviewed using exported assets and the production renderer.
  In-game testing remains pending.

Search, liveries and reward handling
- Removed the None label inside the no-livery tile while retaining selection.
- Restricted model search to normal vehicle browsing in the main showroom.
- Cheat codes work with either Model ID or Model Name search selected.
- Improved reward confirmation routing and resolution of the reward model.
- Reward claims avoid carrying unrelated pending customization charges.
- Reward failures display their actual error on the Congratulations screen.
- Improved reward outdoor-area checks and rejection diagnostics.
- Actual reward spawning still requires in-game verification.

REALBIG return selection and enlarged thumbnails
- Reopening after a reward claim restores the vehicle highlighted before REALBIG.
- The return lookup uses the original model ID to handle catalogue reordering.
- Retained the browsing sort, country/class filters and Favorites filter
  through the reward sequence and reopening.
- Enlarged main-showroom thumbnail rectangles by 30% in Default and Prologue.
- Vehicle previews and the selection arrow scale with the enlarged rectangles.
- Repositioned the thumbnail strip to retain space for the main vehicle and footer.
- Adjusted carousel spacing and scrolling limits for eight visible positions.
- Updated the scrollbar proportion to match the enlarged layout.

Validation for these additions
- ASI compiled with warnings treated as errors.
- Standalone production-renderer previews were reviewed and the size approved.
- Automated checks covered selection return, sort/filter retention, thumbnail
  bounds and hit targets at 1920x1080, 1920x1440 and 1280x720.
- Earlier Default comparison frames matched pixel for pixel during the Prologue
  work. The later thumbnail enlargement intentionally changes that interface.
- These checks cover compilation, state behavior and standalone rendering.
  Native GTA integration and actual reward spawning remain unverified.

RENDERING, RESOURCE OWNERSHIP AND PERFORMANCE FIXES

Preview corruption and streaming
- Reworked preview texture ownership to use private texture contents rather
  than relying on game textures whose contents/lifetime can change
- Improved snapshot allocation, rollback and resource cleanup paths
- Addressed wrong textures and mismatched preview parts after extended browsing
- Improved catalogue readiness, add-on discovery and preview failure handling
- Fixed the platecharset texture-copy failure affecting cheesc (15296) and
  busa (15297); both were confirmed visible after the mip-chain fix
- Improved preview support for vehicle component/frame preparation and
  vehicle-specific wheel/part transforms

Shadows, reflections and AO
- Disabled background/environment reflections on all vehicle thumbnails
- Strengthened thumbnail shadows
- Fixed thumbnail shadow suppression/flicker caused by stale stencil masks:
  each thumbnail now clears both depth and stencil before drawing its shadow
- Added capability-tested anti-aliasing and private depth/normal-based AO
  with quality levels and fallback behavior
- Kept contact shading separate from the moving cast shadow
- Added geometry-based enclosure AO so hidden opaque cabin geometry can
  contribute to shading on seats, dashboards, footwells and enclosed surfaces
- Preserved the existing exterior screen-space AO path and overall AO cap
- Interior AO does not artificially darken material colors or alter textures
- Added bounded, pose-aware enclosure processing and cleanup/failure fallback

Performance and compatibility
- Fixed the major main-Customization-menu slowdown through caching/reuse of
  its prepared presentation data
- Improved private render-target, graphics-state and resource ownership
- Added auxiliary-pass detection for Proper Shaders capture passes
- Improved handling of render hooks shared with other native mods
- Addressed inactive/free-roam state handling and unwanted HUD/radar hiding
- Improved startup/opening readiness checks and restoration paths
- These compatibility changes do not guarantee compatibility with every
  graphics mod, vehicle conversion or executable edition
